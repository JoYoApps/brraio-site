**Thu 12 Nov: Fast rhythms (tachy-1 … tachy-6)**
> Fast rhythm? Two questions: narrow or wide, regular or irregular.
> Narrow + regular: SVT or flutter (if it's stuck on 150, hunt for flutter waves)
> Narrow + irregular: AF
> Wide + regular: VT until proven otherwise
> Wide + irregular: AF with a bundle branch block, or pre-excited AF
