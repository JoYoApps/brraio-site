Common questions

What is Brraio?

Brraio is an ECG practice app for medical staff. It starts with a short ECG Baseline, builds a personalised learning plan around your gaps, and adds new questions, case studies and a weekly challenge every Monday.

Who is Brraio for?

Paramedics, nurses, doctors, students, EMTs, volunteers and first responders. Beginners get an Essentials plan, and experienced clinicians can push themselves with Challenge Me.

Does Brraio count towards CPD?

Brraio logs your learning time and reflections and gives you certificates for your portfolio. It is a self-directed study resource, not an accredited course, so you decide what counts towards your Board's CPD standard.

How much does Brraio cost?

It's free for 3 days with no card needed, then $9.99 AUD a month, or $100 AUD a year. Cancel any time. Join during your trial and your first week as a member is free.

Do I need to download an app?

No. Brraio works in your phone's browser, and you can add it to your home screen so it works like an app, even offline.

Is my information safe?

We only ask for your first name and email, to run your account, send your code and certificates, and (if you choose) send news. We never sell or share your details. Ask us to delete your account any time using the contact form.
